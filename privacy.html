SimFolio Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SimFolio ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

SimFolio is designed with privacy as a core principle. All content you create within the App—including photos, portfolio entries, notes, and organizational data—is stored exclusively on your device. We do not have access to, collect, or store any of this content on our servers.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google, to collect certain technical information automatically. This includes:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers)
  • App usage data (features used, session duration, screens viewed)
  • Crash reports and performance data (error logs, stack traces, app state at time of crash)
  • General location data (country or region level only, not precise location)

This information is collected in an anonymized or pseudonymized form and is used solely to improve app stability and user experience. It does not include any content you create within the App.

1.3 Subscription Information

If you purchase a subscription, payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, process, or store any payment information, credit card numbers, or billing details. We receive only confirmation of your subscription status from Apple to provide you with access to premium features.

2. How We Use Information

The limited technical information we collect through Firebase is used exclusively to:

  • Identify and fix crashes and bugs
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Understand which features are most useful to users
  • Make informed decisions about future app development

We do not use your information for advertising purposes, and we do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All portfolio content, images, and user-created data are stored locally on your device. This means:

  • Your data remains under your control at all times
  • We cannot access, view, or recover your content
  • If you delete the App or lose your device, your locally stored data will be lost unless you have created your own backups

You are solely responsible for backing up any data you wish to preserve.

3.2 Analytics Data

Technical and analytics data collected through Firebase is stored on Google's servers in accordance with Google's privacy practices. This data is retained for a limited period and is subject to Google's data security measures. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google): For app analytics and crash reporting. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: For subscription management. Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: For payment processing. Privacy Policy

These services may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review these policies.

The App does not transmit, store, or process Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).

Any images or information you choose to store in the App are maintained solely on your personal device under your exclusive control. You are solely responsible for:

  • The content you capture and store
  • Ensuring any images you take comply with applicable laws, regulations, and institutional policies
  • Obtaining any necessary consents before photographing patients or clinical work
  • How you use, share, or export any content from the App

We do not access, transmit, or have any involvement with the content of images or data you store locally. If you choose to share or export content from the App through external means (such as email, messaging apps, or cloud services), you are responsible for ensuring such sharing complies with all applicable privacy laws and regulations.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your information:

  • Local Data: You have full control over all locally stored content. You can delete any or all content at any time through the App or by deleting the App itself.
  • Analytics Opt-Out: You can limit analytics data collection by adjusting your device settings or by disabling analytics within the App settings (if available).
  • Data Requests: If you wish to request information about or deletion of any data we may have collected through analytics, please contact us at the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

SimFolio is not intended for use by children under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 17, we will take steps to delete such information.

Your continued use of the App after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
